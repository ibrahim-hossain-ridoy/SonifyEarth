## 1. Executive Summary

This audit verifies that the complete redesign of the **SonifyEarth — Silence Isn't Zero.** web application into a 9-section cinematic scientific storytelling experience (`Home`, `The Mission`, `NASA Visualization`, `Data Journey`, `Global Explorer`, `Timeline`, `Listen`, `Evidence Inspector`, `Data & Methodology`) retained **100% of all existing scientific information, NASA records, archival census metrics, quality-control rules, exclusion audits, listening-mode compatibility mappings, validation reports, and interactive capabilities**.

Nothing was silently deleted, simplified away, or hardcoded over the real NASA data.
